# Templates, Checklists and Tools

> **CHAPTER PURPOSE** Provide a practical artifact toolkit for teams to frame outcomes, design systems, evaluate quality, govern risk and operate services.

The toolkit is intentionally modular. Teams may combine adjacent artifacts when the decision remains clear. Each template below states its minimum useful content.

## 1. Opportunity Assessment

Problem, affected workflow, outcome, baseline availability, stakeholders, intelligence fit, dependencies, risks, horizon and recommendation.

## 2. Outcome Charter

Outcome statement, population, baseline, target, causal hypothesis, scope, exclusions, owner, guardrails, timeline and next gate.

## 3. Outcome Contract

Charter plus service responsibilities, measurement, data sources, review cadence, remedies, funding and renewal/exit conditions.

## 4. Outcome Metric Tree

Primary outcome, leading indicators, operational drivers, guardrails, definitions, sources, segmentation and thresholds.

## 5. Value and Risk Case

Benefits, build/run/intervention/failure costs, uncertainty, impact, autonomy, data, exposure, reversibility and mitigation.

## 6. Process and Decision Map

Actors, steps, systems, inputs, decisions, rules, evidence, queues, exceptions, pain points and outcome events.

## 7. Human–AI Workflow Blueprint

AI role, human role, authority, evidence, interface, approval, override, fallback, escalation, feedback and outcome.

## 8. Data and Knowledge Readiness Assessment

Sources, owners, authority, quality, coverage, freshness, metadata, access, lineage, retention and operations.

## 9. Evaluation Strategy and Dataset

Quality dimensions, cases, sources, splits, expected behavior, graders, thresholds, regression and production sampling.

## 10. Failure Taxonomy

Failure family, severity, detectability, cause, evidence, impact, owner, containment, correction and regression case.

## 11. Intelligence-System Blueprint

Boundary, actors, models, context, knowledge, tools, workflow, memory/state, controls, runtime, telemetry and dependencies.

## 12. Autonomy Matrix

Action, case class, risk, system capability, human authority, limits, evidence threshold, escalation and suspension.

## 13. Production Readiness Checklist

Architecture, security, privacy, evaluations, integration, resilience, monitoring, support, runbook, rollback and approvals.

## 14. Operational Acceptance Checklist

Live users, task completion, quality, controls, adoption, fallback, incidents, training, service owner and accepted authority.

## 15. Outcome Scorecard

Outcome, adoption, intelligence, risk, service, economics, reuse, trend, threshold, commentary and action.

## 16. Service Runbook

Purpose, dependencies, dashboards, alerts, triage, fallbacks, escalation, recovery, changes, contacts and evidence retention.

## 17. Risk and Control Register

Risk, cause, impact, inherent rating, controls, tests, residual rating, owner, treatment, evidence and review.

## 18. Scale and Productization Assessment

Demand, repeatability, consumers, contract stability, configuration, tenancy, support, economics, risk and roadmap.

## 19. Responsibility Assignment Matrix

Activity and decision mapped to accountable, responsible, consulted and informed roles.

## 20. Decision-Gate Record

Decision, evidence, alternatives, conditions, residual risk, owner, due date, expiry and follow-up.

## Template use rule

> **PRACTICALITY** Start with the decision to be made. Complete only the fields required to make, evidence and revisit that decision.
